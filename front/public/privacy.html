Privacy Policy

Meetings App is a student project built for a university course.

What we collect

When you sign in with email and password or with Google, we receive your email address and, for Google, your name. We also store the meetings and participants you create.

How we use it

This data is used only to sign you in and to show you your own meetings. We do not sell it, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored in Amazon Web Services (Cognito and a PostgreSQL database in the us-east-1 region). To have your data deleted, contact: anna2008.ring@gmail.com.
